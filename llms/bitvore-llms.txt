# Bitvore

> Bitvore Corp. runs Cellenus, an AI/NLP platform that reads unstructured public text — global news,
> press releases, SEC filings, earnings-call transcripts — and turns it into structured material business
> events, signals, sentiment, growth and risk scores for corporate entities and municipal bond issuers.
> The platform surveils 500,000+ companies across 60,000+ sources and is sold as two separately licensed
> datasets: Cellenus Corporate Intelligence and Cellenus Municipal (Muni News), with an ESG signal layer.
> Bitvore's fixed-income data-analytics unit was acquired by BondWave in November 2024; bitvore.com now
> redirects to bondwave.com/muni-news/, while developer.bitvore.com and api.bitvore.com remain live.

## Authentication

Three accepted forms, all keyed on a licensed API key:

1. Query parameter `key=<apikey>` — GET requests and dataset exports only.
2. HTTP header `X-BV-APIKEY: <apikey>` — GET and POST; REQUIRED for POST.
3. OAuth 2.0 bearer token (RFC 6750) — `POST /oauth/accesstoken` with
   `grant_type=client_credentials&client_id=<username>&client_secret=<apikey>`.
   No refresh token is issued; request a new token on expiry.

The API key carries product scope: Corporate and Municipal are separately licensed and issue separate keys.
Keys are not self-serve — a trial key comes from products@bitvore.com, a licensed key from sales@bitvore.com.

## Conventions

- Every response is wrapped: `{ success, response, returned, total }` on success,
  `{ success: false, reason, reasonSupport, response }` on failure.
- Pagination is page-number: `pageNo` + `pageSize`, with `returned` and `total` in the envelope.
- Date ranges use required `startDate`/`endDate` ISO 8601 values plus optional `dateType` and `tz`.
- Multi-value parameters are plural (`bvIds`, `signals`) as of v2.
- Primary identifier is `bvId`; CUSIP, ISIN, SEDOL and FIGI are licensed alternates.
- No idempotency key, no request-id header, no published rate limit, no RFC 9457 problem details.
